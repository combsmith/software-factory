## Question

When a workflow run has uploaded an artifact of one file, and another of two, what does GitHub answer to the list of the run's artifacts by a name (`GET /repos/{repo}/actions/runs/{id}/artifacts?name=...&per_page=100`), for a name that is there, a name that is not, and after the artifact is deleted; to its download (`GET /repos/{repo}/actions/artifacts/{id}/zip`); and what does the storage the download redirects to answer?

## Pass

The list gives `total_count` and, for each artifact, `id`, `name`, `size_in_bytes`, `expired` and `digest`, the digest as `sha256:` and 64 hex digits, or absent; `size_in_bytes` is the length of the zip the storage serves and `digest` its sha256; the download answers with a redirect to GitHub's artifact storage, a host `tools/check_repo.py`'s `STORAGE` names, and the storage answers 200 with the zip; the zip of a one-file artifact holds that file under its own name and nothing else; and `adapters/artifacts.py` reads the recorded exchange whole with no change (issue 917).
