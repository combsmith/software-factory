# P-38

Batch: 2

## Question

When GitHub dismisses an approving review as stale on a push, does the review stay on the commit it approved, readable with that commit and its reviewer; and who does the timeline record as dismissing it?

## Pass

It stays, with its commit, and the dismissal names its actor: the contract a person approved is read at that commit (decision 0025). If it does not stay, the port reads the approval from somewhere that does, before any contract is in force. **Passed** on 2026-10-31 (`trials/P-38.json`) for a person's push, and P-7 for an App's: each approval stays on its commit with its reviewer, and the timeline names the pusher and the push's commit, with no message
