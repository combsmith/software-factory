# P-64

Batch: 4

## Question

When a Run waits in its item's lane behind another, does the jobs API's `started_at` for its job say when the job left the queue and began, later than the run's `run_started_at` by the time it waited, and what does a read of its job give while it waits?

## Pass

Yes: a job's `started_at`, once it is in progress, is when it began, so a record's start can be read from its job and not from `run_started_at`, which is the run's creation (P-44; issue 633). The records port reads it as a record's `began_at` (decision 0193), and the approval's anchor takes a record as old as a browser's approval by it (decision 0204), which closes record 0158's residual of a Run made while the act ran and started later. **Passed** on 2026-11-10 (`trials/P-64.json`): of two runs ten seconds apart in one group, the second waited 53 seconds; its job's `started_at` was 19:44:48Z, a second after the first job completed, and stood at every later read, while the run's `run_started_at` stayed 19:43:55Z, its creation. While the job waited, `pending`, the jobs API gave its `started_at` as its creation too: a job's `started_at` says when it began only once its status is `in_progress`, or `completed` with a runner named. A job cancelled or skipped while it waited ends `completed` with no runner and no step and never began (P-44's cancelled jobs), so its `started_at` is no start either
