# P-37

Batch: 2

## Question

For a merge-queue entry behind another, is `github.event.merge_group.base_sha` the commit its group is built on, the head of the group ahead of it, and not the tip of `main`; and is it, every time, the parent of the group's own head?

## Pass

Yes to both: the base a Lens reads from is what `main` will be when the entry merges (decision 0023). If it is the tip of `main`, an entry behind another is judged against rules the entry ahead may change, and is judged again once that one merges. **Passed** on 2026-10-31 (`trials/P-37.json`), read only from the factory's own queue, which builds five groups at once. In all 228 groups the branch's commit is the head's one parent. In all 35 groups built while the group ahead still ran whose log says so, `merge_group.base_sha` is that commit, the head of the group ahead; 15 older ones have no such log line
