# NNNN. A Result's reason is one of the Engine's codes, and a Finding's lines are on a hunk of its file at the head

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's ruling of 2026-11-05 on the story that follows the harness review's, "A Result's reason is one of the Engine's codes, and a Finding's lines are on the head's side of the diff" (issue 567), before the first live Run, and of 2026-11-06 on its codes: the two the model may write, as proposed, with the prompt left as it is, a counter-example for each way a reason may not suit its status, and the schema handed to the CLI narrowed from `result/1`. It adds to decision 0038 a list of the codes a Result's `reason` may be, and to the reasons `apply` refuses a Result two more; and it reads the lines decision 0137 numbers as the hunks a Finding may sit on. Nothing else in either changes.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A Result's reason | `result/1` holds `reason` to null or one of twelve codes, and no other: the two the model may write, `change_not_reviewed_whole` with `incomplete` and `task_not_a_review` with `refused`, and the ten the Harness writes with a `failed` Result it writes whole, `credential_missing`, `engine_version`, `task_too_large`, `engine_failed`, `engine_timeout`, `no_answer` and `result_invalid` in `harness/engine.py`, and `diff_not_whole`, `head_unreadable` and `rubric_unreadable` in `harness/run.py`. The lists are `harness/engine.py`'s `MODEL_REASONS` and `HARNESS_REASONS`. The schema handed to the CLI narrows `reason` to null and the model's two, as it narrows `status`, reading them from `result/1`'s list and never from a copy of it: `enum` is a keyword P-13 proved, but a null inside it is new to that schema, and the canary is the first Run to show the CLI takes it | A reason is a code, never a sentence, and a code the Factory never named is one no reader can act on. `apply` copies the reason into `applied/1` and `outcome/1` |
| A reason its status may give | `apply` refuses a Result whose reason is not one its status may give, the model's on a `failed` Result or the Harness's on an `incomplete` or `refused` one, as `reason_not_the_status`, the Role's (`item`). `apply` takes a Result that did not complete and gives no reason, as before, though the Engine refuses such an answer (decision 0038); a completed Result gives none, of anyone's; and a `superseded` Result, which nothing writes yet, may give none of these codes. The one rule, `harness/engine.py`'s `reason_given`, is also the Engine's, so an answer that breaks it is `result_invalid`, and `tools/check_examples.py`'s, so a counter-example of each way is refused there, as no schema here can say it | The Harness writes a `failed` Result and the model never does, so a model's code on one, or the Harness's on the model's status, did not come from where it says |
| The model's two codes | The two follow the words `roles/pr-review/prompt.md` already gives each status: `incomplete` "when the change could not be reviewed whole", `refused` "when the Task is not a review you can make". The prompt is left as it is, as a model Lens's input another story is changing; the CLI is held to the codes by the schema it is handed. A Role that needs another code adds it under a record of its own | The codes say what the prompt says, and no more |
| A Finding's lines | `apply` refuses a Result that posts a Finding, as a review comment or as an annotation, whose lines from `start_line` to `end_line` are not all on one hunk of its file on the head's side, as `finding_not_in_hunk`, the Role's. The hunks are read from the Task's own diff, the text of kind `diff` the model was given (decision 0137): each file by its name after `+++ `, read once before its first hunk, a name git quoted unescaped and one with a space, quoted or not, without git's tab, the first such line naming it even where that name is `/dev/null` or cannot be read, and each hunk's lines from its header's first line and count on the head's side. A file the diff deletes, which decision 0145 takes as a place on the change, a hunk that only removes, a file the Task's diff left out, and a name that is no UTF-8 hold no line. Another text's body gives none, however it reads | GitHub refuses a review comment off the diff, and the review is then refused whole, but an annotation it takes wherever it is placed. A Finding names lines of the change the model was shown, and the Task's diff is what it was shown. The facts carry the files and their count, not the hunks |
| Where `apply` reads the diff | `apply` is not handed the Task, only what the Decision admitted, so the apply step reads the item's diff again, as the read step reads it, at the head the facts were read at (a pull request moved between the two is `superseded`, `head_moved`), and `apply` builds the Task's diff again from those bytes, cut at the Kernel's own `max_diff_characters` as the Run's Task was. Bytes that do not hash to the content admitted are `superseded`, `content_moved`, and nothing is written | The lines are the ones the model was shown, and the bytes are held to the content admitted, as the read step holds them. It is three requests more, the pull request, its base and the compare, for each Result that posts a Finding, on an item the facts show still open, unheld and unpaused on the content admitted, within the reads the Role App is granted (`read:facts.diff`). A change under the Run is told first, by the facts alone and in `refusal`'s order, and the diff is read only after: so a closed item is `item_closed` and moved content `content_moved`, and no failed or moved read of the diff can make one the environment's, which counts toward a pause |
| The cost | A review is given the head to read too (decision 0138), so a model may place a Finding on a line it read there that no hunk holds, and the whole Result is then refused, its other Findings with it. `roles/pr-review/prompt.md` says only that a Finding's lines are the numbers the diff gives, and `tools/eval_result.py` scores a Finding's place by its overlap and does not apply this rule, so the eval gate does not show such a refusal. A Finding on what a deletion breaks is placed on lines of the change at the head, never on a deleted file's lines, which no review comment the Factory posts can sit on. Telling the model so, and scoring the rule in the eval, are a story of their own, as another story is changing the prompt now | Named so that a refused Result is not taken for a bug, and so the eval's gap is known |
| A diff cut short | A reviewing Intent whose Task leaves part of the diff out is never asked (`diff_not_whole`, "Kernel: a pull request whose diff the Task cannot carry whole is held", issue 327). Any other Run whose Task leaves a file out may post no Finding on it | It fails closed |

## Why

`result/1` held `reason` to lower-case letters and underscores, with no list and no bound, so a model could write any code and `apply` would copy it into what the Factory records. And `start_line` and `end_line` were bounded to nine digits but not held to the change, so an annotation could be placed on any line of any file. Each is now held to what the Factory itself named or showed: a reason to the Engine's codes, by the status that gives it, and a Finding's lines to a hunk of the diff in the Task.
