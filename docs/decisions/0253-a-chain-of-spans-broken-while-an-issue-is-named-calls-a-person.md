# 0253. A chain of spans broken while an issue is named calls a person

Status: accepted. Extends decision 0234 with its part 5 for "Once issues are served, each touched open issue is re-read every pass with no budget of its own, and a short list stops the whole pass" (issue 849): a chain broken while the issue is named, the view of an issue left unread, and the queue past a short list, as the Factory Builder ruled on 2026-11-19 (B1 as C, B3, B4) and the Solution Architect on 2026-11-21. Decision 0234 gains only the Boundary row that says its row is held

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Which first | The issues left out as `issue_budget` by the newest pass record that read the issues are read first, as decision 0251 orders them: a record whose list was read short, which read no issue, is passed over (ruling B4) | A short list, which an outsider can force, must not empty the queue |
| A chain broken while named | The issue's facts say so, `spans_broken` in `issue-facts/1`, where the records break the chain while a pull request names the issue or anything but an issue cross-references it. The issues' Kernel defers it `spans_broken`, after a read cut at its bound and before any list read short, and calls a person. The flag is not required: absent, or anything but exact true, it is read as not broken, and the linked list read short defers the issue all the same (ruling C) | No later pass mends such a chain, so it must not wait in silence. It covers an issue left out as `facts_unanswered` or `pull_moved` too |
| The view | An issue a pass left unread for want of a read alone, its budget spent or its list read short, is shown in the view as the record before decided it, where it did, one pass deep; and its time in its State passes over such passes (ruling B3) | Before the budget, a short list stopped the pass and the old view stood. One pass deep keeps the view equal to its rebuild |

## Why

Decisions 0251 and 0252 leave issues out by a budget and a short list. Each such pass is one the keyword spans' chain, the view and the queue must read past without breaking in silence; this part makes them do so, and calls a person where the chain is broken for good.
