# 0143. An eval case's fault may be rightly placed at more than one place, and each is named blind

Status: accepted. The Maintainer's choice of 2026-11-05, and his ruling of the same day on a Finding's span, after a private audit of the held-out set's misses: some faults have more than one place a careful reviewer could rightly put them, and a case's answer names only one. It adds to `eval-case/1`, `tools/eval_result.py`, `eval-report/1` and the eval job's report, and `maxItems` to `tools/jsonschema_lite.py`. The gate, its bar and each kind's bar (decision 0120) do not change.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| The case | An expected Finding may carry `also_at`, a list of one to three places, each with its own path and its `start_line`, and an `end_line` where it spans more than one. Absent, the Finding has one place, its `location`, as before. An empty list, a fourth place, an entry with no path, or one with no `start_line`, fails the schema | A fault that spans several changed lines, or several files of one change, is rightly placed on any of them: the line that brings it in, the line that uses it, the file that causes it. A key that names one of them fails a reviewer who chose another for no fault of its reading |
| The score | A raised Finding is at the expected place when it overlaps `location` or any entry of `also_at`, by the rule `location` is read by. That holds for the Finding that cites the criterion, and, for one that does not, for whether it is at the place, `finding_uncited` or `finding_cites_another`, or in a file of one of them but elsewhere, `finding_elsewhere` | One fault, several places: every other place the scorer looks must agree with the first, or a Finding passes on one reading and is said elsewhere on another |
| A Finding's span | A raised Finding is at a place, its `location` or one of `also_at`, only where it gives its `start_line` and spans no more than fifteen lines beyond the place's own span. One with no lines, or wider, is elsewhere: `location_differs` where it cites the criterion, `finding_elsewhere` where it does not. A case's `location` given without lines is still the whole file | An unbounded or line-less Finding could be at any place, and a Role drifting toward broad Findings would score higher while being less use to whoever reads them. Fifteen lines leave room for a Finding that takes in its function's head or its test |
| Inside the diff | Each place of `also_at` must lie, every line of it, inside one hunk of the case's own diff, on the head's side, in the file whose `+++ b/` header names it: the lines the Task numbers for the model (decision 0137). A place on a line the head's side does not hold, across the gap between two hunks, or in a file the diff does not change, makes the case `location_unreadable`. So does a file whose header git quotes, as it does a path of other than plain characters: it is no `+++ b/` header. A case whose diff the Task would cut is stopped `diff_not_whole` before it is scored | An alternate place is a changed line the reviewer was shown, never a line elsewhere that a key could stretch to meet any answer. A path alone, which would be the whole file, is refused for the same reason, and three places are enough for a fault the audit can justify |
| The mark | A pass whose Finding was at a place of `also_at` alone, and no right Finding at its `location`, is scored with `at_alternate`, true. The eval job carries it into the report as a case's `at_alternate`, which its run line counts, and the gate refuses a report that marks a case not passed | The report can say how much of a score the alternates gave, by a mark that names no place, so a rise in the score can be read against it |
| What is unchanged | Category, severity and criterion are the case's one answer, whichever place the Finding is at. A Finding must still be the expected one in every respect | A place is where the fault can be read; what the fault is does not move with it |
| A place unreadable | Every place a case gives must be readable: an entry of `also_at` whose end comes before its start makes the case `location_unreadable`, as its `location` does | A place that cannot be read would be read as nowhere, and a case would pass or fail on what its key cannot say |
| How places are added | By an audit of the private set that does not see the model's answers, to every case alike, each justified on its own diff in the private home. Never to the cases a run failed alone | A place added where the model put its Finding would mark the model's answer right because it was the model's |
| The cost | A looser key can only raise the score: a place added never fails a Finding that passed. So `also_at` moves the bar closer for every case it is given to, and the gate measures less than it did unless each place is one a careful reviewer would rightly choose. Whether each is, is not checked here, since no session here reads a case | Named so that it is not mistaken for a free gain. It is why places are added blind, to every case, and justified case by case where the cases are |

## Why

The private audit found misses where the review raised the right fault, rightly placed, at a line or file the key did not name. A key that names every right place measures the review's judgment rather than its luck in choosing the one place written down. The bounds narrow what a key may say and what an answer may claim: three places at most, each on the lines the reviewer was shown; a Finding held to its place's span and fifteen lines more; and a mark on every pass the alternates gave, so neither a looser key nor a broader Finding can raise the score unseen. Several narrow Findings that cite the one criterion are another matter, which this record does not bound: a case is passed by its nearest citing Finding, as before it. The scorer, the schema and the rule for adding places are here; the places themselves, and any count of them, stay in the private home.
