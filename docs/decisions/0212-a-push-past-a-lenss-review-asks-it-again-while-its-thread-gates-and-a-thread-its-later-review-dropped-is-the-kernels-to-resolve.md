# 0212. A push past a Lens's review asks it again while its thread gates, and a thread its later review dropped is the Kernel's to resolve

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's rulings of 2026-11-13 on "A gating thread the reviewer opens is closed only by a person's hand: no Role answers it, no Intent rules on it and no queue App resolves it" (issue 833), the Maintainer's priority. It adds a way to decision 0086's row "When a Lens's thread is closed", as decision 0125 did for an expired Run's thread. The Maintainer may overturn it in review.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A push that leaves the diff as it was | While a required model Lens's own thread gates and still stands, a push after that Lens's last review of this content began asks its review again (`awaiting_review`). A merge of the base is such a push: the same content, a new head. A review that began in the push's own second does not answer it. A thread the Kernel is already to resolve asks nothing, nor does a person's, another Lens's, or one that gates nothing; nor does a push at no time that can be read. The Run ceiling bounds it, as any review. A push that changes the diff is new content, reviewed as before | A Finding may be answered by what the head now holds and the diff does not show. On "Trial P-66, a queued pull request: once the base it waits on moves into conflict with it, the queue takes it out unmerged" (pull request 847) the reviewer asked for a file that reached the head by a merge of `main`, and nothing reviewed again |
| Which thread is said dropped | `adapters/applied.py` says a Lens's thread is `dropped` where it is in the threads read, opened by that Lens, and every Run that recorded its Finding is one a later review of that Lens supersedes by decision 0075's rule. It is matched by the thread's id in the Runs' records, never by a comment's text, and keeps its category, severity and ruling. `facts/1` carries the word as a yes, or leaves it out | `apply` posts each gating Finding of a Run on a thread of its own, so what a later review raises again is on a new thread, which gates |
| How the Kernel reads it | `kernel/derive.py`'s `as_read` marks such a thread `superseded` only where it opened before the last push and the run records hold a review by that Lens that succeeded on this content and began after that push: a review of a head newer than the thread's. Marked, it is read as decision 0125 reads an expired Run's thread: the Kernel's `resolve_thread` act (`resolve_due`, State adjudicating), after a person's gate and before the author is sent back, closed by the queue App's resolution or a person's. `kernel/gate.py` is as it was | That review is the Lens's judgement of the change as it now is |
| What is not read as dropped | With no push since the thread opened, as when a Run that failed is tried again, the thread gates as before. So does one whose category the Kernel does not know, and one its Lens's latest ruling rejected. After a later push the thread stands again, and its review is asked for again. A word that is neither a yes nor a no is `thread_unreadable` | Only a newer head gives the Lens something new to judge, and what cannot be read is never read as closed (I-13) |
| A security Finding | Its thread is never closed this way, and the act never names one, whatever ruling the facts carry | A security Finding is never accepted as declined or deferred (decisions 0007 and 0086) |
| What does not close it | A resolution by the Lens's own App, the machine user, the author's App, another Lens's App or a stranger, dropped or not; and a review by another Lens, one that failed, an adjudication, or a review of other content drops nothing | Decisions 0075 and 0086, unchanged |
| Who carries out the act in slice 1 | Until `config/controller.json` holds the queue App's account ("A narrow queue App, combsmith-sf-queue, enqueues and dequeues with contents and pull-request write alone (P-2)", issue 381) the pass records the act `not_built`. In that time a session may resolve a Lens's thread as the Maintainer's account only where the Controller's check on the pull request's current head says `resolve_due`, its act `resolve_thread` naming that thread; the thread is no security Finding's and no expired Run's, whose category cannot be read; and the session posts a comment naming the pass and the thread | It carries out the Kernel's act, as an enqueue by hand does: the Lens dropped the Finding, and the Kernel issued the act (L-17) |
| What holds the hand | A test holds what the act may name. No check holds the hand's part: "No check holds the hand's part of resolving a Lens's thread in slice 1: nothing notices a resolution the Kernel's act did not name" (issue 854) | The gate reads any resolution by a person with write access as closing a Lens's thread |
| What a later review's silence means | The review is given none of its Lens's open Findings, so its silence is one more review of the change and no ruling on that Finding: "A re-review is given none of its Lens's open Findings, so a thread closes on the silence of one more review" (issue 856) | Decision 0075 accepts the same for a thread that is gone |
| The fake | Not built: "The simulator and the transitions explorer never push a head that leaves the diff as it was, and never say a thread dropped" (issue 855) | The world's every push is new content |
| The merge | Main's ruleset requires every thread resolved, and the merge queue refuses an enqueue with one open (decision 0089). So such a pull request converges once the thread is resolved, and not before | The ruleset is not changed here |

## Why

Once the reviewer's own account was listed as a Lens, each blocking or major Finding it posted held its pull request for a person. A later review by the same Lens already released a pull request held by a thread that was gone, and closed an expired Run's thread. Of a newer head, it says as much of a thread that is still there; and a push that answers a Finding without changing the diff now gets that review.
