# 0212. A push past a Lens's review asks it again while its thread gates, and a thread its later review dropped is the Kernel's to resolve

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's rulings of 2026-11-13 on "A gating thread the reviewer opens is closed only by a person's hand: no Role answers it, no Intent rules on it and no queue App resolves it" (issue 833), the Maintainer's priority. It adds a way to decision 0086's row "When a Lens's thread is closed", as decision 0125 did for an expired Run's thread. The Maintainer may overturn it in review.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A push that leaves the diff as it was | While a required model Lens's own thread gates and still stands, a push after that Lens's last review of this content began asks its review again (`awaiting_review`). A merge of the base is such a push: the same content, a new head. A review that began in the push's own second does not answer it. A thread the Kernel is already to resolve asks nothing, nor does a person's, another Lens's, or one that gates nothing. A push that changes the diff is new content, reviewed as before | A Finding may be answered by what the head now holds and the diff does not show. On "Trial P-66, a queued pull request: once the base it waits on moves into conflict with it, the queue takes it out unmerged" (pull request 847) the reviewer asked for a file that reached the head by a merge of `main`, and nothing reviewed again |
| What bounds it | The Run ceiling, as any review: one Run for each push while a thread stands | No new count |
| Which thread is said dropped | `adapters/applied.py` says a Lens's thread is `dropped` where it is in the threads read, opened by that Lens, and every Run that recorded its Finding is one a later review of that Lens supersedes by decision 0075's rule: a review that succeeded, read whole, on the current content, begun after them. It is matched by the thread's id in the Runs' records, never by a comment's text, and keeps its category, severity and ruling. `facts/1` carries the word as a yes, or leaves it out | `apply` posts each gating Finding of a Run on a thread of its own, so what a later review raises again is on a new thread, which gates. The earlier thread is one it did not record |
| How the Kernel reads it | `kernel/derive.py`'s `as_read` holds the port's word to the run records: it marks the thread `superseded` only where they hold a review by that Lens that succeeded on this content and began after the thread opened. Marked, it is read as decision 0125 reads an expired Run's thread once its Lens has reviewed again: the Kernel's `resolve_thread` act (`resolve_due`, State adjudicating), after a person's gate and before the author is sent back, closed by the queue App's resolution or a person's. `kernel/gate.py` is as it was | The later review is the Lens's judgement of the change as it now is |
| A word the records do not bear out | A thread said dropped with no such review in the records, or whose opening time cannot be read, contradicts them: while open it is `thread_unreadable`. So is a word that is neither a yes nor a no. One a person closed is read past | The Kernel derives a State from the facts and the run records together (I-13) |
| A security Finding | Its thread is never closed this way. Said dropped, it gates as before, and the queue App's resolution closes nothing | A security Finding is never accepted as declined or deferred (decisions 0007 and 0086). Its category can be read here, as an expired Run's cannot |
| What does not close it | The Lens's own App, the machine user, the author's App, another Lens's App or a stranger resolving the thread closes nothing, dropped or not. A review by another Lens drops nothing, nor does one that failed, an adjudication, or a review of other content | Decisions 0075 and 0086, unchanged |
| Who carries out the act in slice 1 | Until `config/controller.json` holds the queue App's account ("A narrow queue App, combsmith-sf-queue, enqueues and dequeues with contents and pull-request write alone (P-2)", issue 381) the pass records the act `not_built`. In that time a session may resolve a Lens's thread as the Maintainer's account only where the Controller's check on the pull request's current head says `resolve_due`, its act `resolve_thread` naming that thread; the thread is no security Finding's; and the session posts a comment naming the pass and the thread, as the record of a hand act | It carries out the Kernel's act, as an enqueue by hand does. It is not the author judging its own gate (L-17): the Lens dropped the Finding, and the Kernel issued the act |
| The merge | Main's ruleset requires every thread resolved, and the merge queue refuses an enqueue with one open (decision 0089). So such a pull request converges once the thread is resolved, and not before | The ruleset is not changed here |

## Why

Once the reviewer's own account was listed as a Lens, each blocking or major Finding it posted held its pull request for a person: no Role answers a Finding in slice 1, and nothing but a person's resolution closed its thread. A later review by the same Lens already released a pull request held by a thread that was gone, and already closed an expired Run's thread. It says as much of a thread that is still there; and a push that answers a Finding without changing the diff now gets that review.
