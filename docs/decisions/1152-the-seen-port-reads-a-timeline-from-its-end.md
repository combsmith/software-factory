# 1152. The seen port reads a timeline from its end

Status: accepted. Extends decision 0234 with the seen port's read of a timeline from its end, as "adapters: bound a pull request's timeline and seen-issue reads by pages" (issue 1009) asks, part 4 of 4, as the Factory Builder ruled on 2026-11-20 (A, with its guard). It restates the rows of decision 1150 that it widens, now that a cut timeline's writes are read from its end; decisions 0234 and 1150 stand as they are.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A pull request's own reads | The facts port reads a pull request's own timeline, and its reviews, to ten pages each (`timeline.bounded`, and `BOUND` in `timeline.reviews`). A tenth page that names an eleventh leaves the list not whole and cut, `timeline_bounded`, and the eleventh is never asked. A review dismissed whose dismissal a timeline not read whole does not hold, as one past its cut, leaves the reviews not whole, never the snapshot refused | Anyone can comment on or review an open pull request, and each was read whole on every pass: the amplification the issues port's bound ends, on the pull request side (the Claude and security reads of the acceptance port's bound) |
| A timeline read from its end | A timeline the seen port reads, an issue's, an item's that left, or one the snapshot cut, it reads from its end: the first page names the last (`rel="last"`, as trials/P-43.json recorded it), and the last pages are read back until one holds a moment before `since`, or the second, which joins the first; ten pages at most, the last read again, and a joined first page too. A last or joined first page that changed by then, as an event landing or a deletion moves the pages, leaves the read not whole; items are kept once each by their kind and id, and each one with no id is kept. One read as gone, whole only with its close of any age, is read on back within the same bound until the close is found | The writes since `since`, and an item's close, are at a timeline's end, so one cut at its start would never be read whole again, and an item that left would be read every pass for good (the same reads, the Factory Builder's ruling (A) of 2026-11-20 with its guard) |

## Why

Decision 1150 cuts a pull request's timeline at ten pages from its start, and decision 1151 cuts the seen port's lists so. The writes since a pass's window opened, and an item's close, are at a timeline's end: read from the start, a cut timeline would never be read whole again. This part reads the seen port's timelines from their end, back to the window, so a long timeline still gives its newest writes.
