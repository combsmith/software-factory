# NNNN. A case that expects a Finding takes one Finding at its place, and one gating Finding away from it

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's story of 2026-11-07, issue 610, "The scorer bounds surplus Findings at and away from the expected place". It bounds what decision 0143 left unbounded: a case is passed by the nearest of the Findings that cite its criterion, so extra Findings cost nothing.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| At the place | On a case that expects a Finding, more than one Finding at the place fails the case by `surplus_at_place`, whatever each cites, its category or its severity. The place is the case's `location` or any entry of `also_at`, as decision 0143 reads it, so one fault put at two of its places is put twice | A Result could put several Findings at the right lines with different criteria, categories and severities, and one of them would match. A Role that hedges over labels at the right place scored as one that judged it |
| Away from the place | More than one gating Finding away from every place fails the case by `surplus_gating_elsewhere`. A gating Finding is one that would hold a pull request, as decision 0144 reads it: blocking or major, or security at any severity. A Finding with no lines, or no location, is at no place (decision 0143), so it is away. Findings that would hold nothing are not counted, however many | One gating Finding away is allowed, for a real fault the case's authors could have missed. More is a Result scattering Findings that would each hold a change |
| What is unchanged | A miss is told as before, beside these codes. A fault of the case, and a Finding off every hunk (decision 0156), are told first, alone, with no count. A clean case is unchanged: any gating Finding already fails it (decision 0144) | The bound adds to the pass rule and moves nothing the scorer already said |
| The report | `eval-report/1` lists both codes among a case's codes, faults of the reviewer's, as any miss is. Each case that expects a Finding carries `surplus_gating`, the count of its gating Findings away from every place, on any verdict, where there was one at least; the run's line sums them | The issue asks the report to count them. A count says no place and no word of the model's, as `at_alternate` does |
| Decision 0143's mark | Unchanged in code. A pass now holds one Finding at the place, the one that cites the criterion, so the mark reads whether that one is at the `location`. The two mutants that put a second Finding at the `location` beside a pass, "an uncited Finding at the location unmarks a pass" and "a wrong Finding at the location unmarks a pass", are removed: no pass can hold one, so no test can tell them from the code | A mutant no input can tell apart is no test of anything |
| The cost | A review that rightly raises two faults on the same lines fails the case, and so does one that rightly raises two gating faults the case's authors missed | A case's place is where its one fault is read, and two missed faults in one case is a case to mend in the private home, not a review to pass by guess. The count in the report shows where the second happens |
| The prompt | Not changed. The review is not told of the bound | The bound asks of the review only what a review should do anyway: one Finding for one fault, and none that would hold a change without one |

## Why

Decision 0143 named the gap: several Findings that cite the one criterion were not bounded, and a case was passed by its nearest. Nor were Findings that cited another criterion at the place, or gating Findings anywhere else on a case that expects one. A Role tuned on this score could learn to hedge. The bound is narrow on purpose: one Finding at the place, since the case holds one fault there, and one gating Finding away from it, since a case's authors can miss a real fault. The held-out pass count may move.
