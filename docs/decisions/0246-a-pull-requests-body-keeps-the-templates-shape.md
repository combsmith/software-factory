# 0246. A pull request's body keeps the template's shape

Status: accepted. The Solution Architect's ruling of 2026-11-21, relayed by the Factory Builder, on the readability lane's plan, item 2, for "tools: check that pull request bodies keep the template's shape" (issue 1100). It adds to decision 0244 and changes none of it.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| The rule | A pull request's body holds the four headings of `.github/pull_request_template.md`, `## Why`, `## What changed`, `## What to look at` and `## Record and protected edits`: each once, in that order, each over some text. Other sections may stand beside them, and the checks-and-readers block is not required | Since 2026-11-21 the squash takes the body as the commit's message, so the body is main's log for good, as the title is (decision 0244) |
| A heading | Up to three spaces, `##`, then nothing or a space or tab and the words, a closing run of hashes dropped only after a space or tab, as GitHub reads a second-level heading; a bare `##` is one of no words, and a section ends at any. LF, CRLF and CR are line ends | The web editor saves CRLF and the desk reads LF; the same body must get the same verdict |
| What is hidden | A heading or a fence counts only on a line that starts outside a hidden part, read as written, a fence before any tag on it; a heading's words are what a reader sees of them, its closing hashes read as written. A fence (three or more backticks or tildes, after any indent or list marker) hides what it holds until one of its kind, at least as long, at most three columns further in than its list item, with only spaces or tabs after; a backtick fence whose words hold a backtick is inline code. A comment, and a `<details>` block of any case, with attributes or nested, hides what it holds, to the end if left open. Outside a comment, a tag in inline code is none | What a body quotes, or hides, is not its own shape. Reading an indented fence as one mostly hides more |
| The limit of the reading | A line at a time, in good faith, for the forms a body written to the template uses. Not read: inline code across lines, a fence in a block or a quote, a list's indent, HTML but comments and `<details>`; inline code is found on the line as written. A body built to hide its shape is out of scope, as with decision 0215's syntax marks | Only the Maintainer's account opens a pull request here: this guards against a mistake, not an attack. Where it differs from GitHub, it mostly hides more, and the desk shows that before opening |
| Some text | Any character but whitespace that a reader sees, and not an empty list item (a bare `-`, `*`, `+`, or a number and `.` or `)`). "None." is text, and so is code in a fence; an empty fence is not. A heading held twice is read for each of its sections | Nothing but a person's words should keep a section, never the template's scaffolding |
| What is judgment | Whether "Why" is plain, and whether "What to look at" is honest. No check holds these | They cannot be told from the text alone |
| Where it is held | `tools/pr_title.py`, inside `check`, from the title's read, by the one call in `tools/commits_are_the_maintainers.py` (decision 0244). The workflow does not change | A second module, call or read would add a thing to protect and nothing to hold |
| What cannot be read | A body that is null, missing or not text refuses with its own code, `body_unreadable` | GitHub answers null for an empty body; a check that passes what it could not read holds nothing |
| The cutover | A pull request opened before 2026-11-23T00:00:00Z, by its `created_at`, is not read for its body. It is still read for its title, from 0244's cutover | The lanes get a day to write their queued bodies to it |
| What is printed | A code and the template's heading names, never a word of the body, by the job and by `python3 -m tools.pr_title --body FILE` | The body is text from outside; the log reads a line starting `::` as a command |
| The limit: a Role's drafts | When a Role is named to open drafts (decision 0215), its bodies must keep the template's shape, or the record that names it must exempt them | A draft's body is the model's (decision 0238) |
| The limits of 0244 | A reopened pull request opened before the cutover stays out of scope; a body edited after a read is read again at the next push or the merge group; what can blind the commits check can blind this one | The body is read by the title's call, at its moments |

## Why

The template made bodies readable when people used it. Since the body is now main's commit message, a body out of shape is a commit message out of shape for good. The mechanical half of the shape, its headings and that each says something, is held where the title already is, at no cost in reads or workflow changes.

## Boundaries

| Boundary | Check |
|---|---|
| The template's headings are the check's; a body in shape passes, "None." is text, other sections and the details block are allowed | `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_keeps_the_templates_shape_or_says_which_part_it_breaks`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "the body needs no Why" |
| Each heading missing, held twice or over nothing a reader sees refuses with its own code; any two swapped refuse as out of order, a heading held twice among them, and each of its sections is read; the untouched template keeps no section | `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_keeps_the_templates_shape_or_says_which_part_it_breaks`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "a missing heading passes", mutant: "a heading held twice passes", mutant: "an empty section passes", mutant: "headings out of order pass" |
| Fences: all four headings inside one refuse; a fence closes only on its own kind, at least as long, at most three columns further in than its list item, with only spaces or tabs after; inline code opens none; a fence in a list item hides what it holds; a fence and a comment hide each other's marks | `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_keeps_the_templates_shape_or_says_which_part_it_breaks`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "a fenced heading counts", mutant: "a fence closes on the other kind", mutant: "a fence closes four spaces in", mutant: "a fence's words open a comment" |
| Comments and details blocks hide their headings, whole, nested, of any case, with attributes, or left open; a comment inside a block hides its tags; one named in inline code hides nothing, inside a block too | `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_keeps_the_templates_shape_or_says_which_part_it_breaks`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "a comment hides nothing", mutant: "a nested block ends at its first close", mutant: "a comment in a block hides nothing", mutant: "a tag named in inline code in a block closes it" |
| A heading is read as GitHub reads one: another level, four spaces or a tab in, a hash run in its words, or a comment before it is none, and a comment after its words is none of them; a bare `##` ends a section; CRLF and CR are line ends | `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_keeps_the_templates_shape_or_says_which_part_it_breaks`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "a heading four spaces in counts", mutant: "a heading after a comment on its line counts", mutant: "a comment after a heading's words is read as words" |
| Every problem is said, none holds a word of the body, and a body of the largest size is read in time | `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_keeps_the_templates_shape_or_says_which_part_it_breaks`, `tests/test_tools.py::test_a_body_of_the_largest_size_is_read_in_time`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "only the first body problem is said", mutant: "a body problem says the body" |
| The body is the one read's, never the event's, on a pull request and on a merge group; a null, missing or non-text body refuses with `body_unreadable`; one opened a second before the cutover is not read for its body, one opened at it is | `tests/test_tools.py::test_the_body_is_read_on_both_events_from_its_cutover`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "the body is not checked on a merge group", mutant: "a body of no text passes", mutant: "a body opened at its cutover is not read", mutant: "the body's cutover is the title's" |
| The desk's command checks a body's file, says what it found, and prints nothing of the body | `tests/test_tools.py::test_the_desk_checks_a_bodys_file`, `tools/pr_title.py`, mutant: "the desk passes every body", mutant: "the desk passes a body it cannot read", mutant: "the desk's body command takes extra files" |
