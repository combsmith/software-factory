# 0224. A second model family reads a change as an advisory Lens inside the review's Run, and `pr-review` adopts only what it checked

Status: accepted. The Maintainer ruled its eight points on 2026-11-13, one question at a time, on the comment of issue 390 ("Design: CodeRabbit runs as an external Lens through its CLI, read as structured Findings, never as comment threads") that puts a second model family on that issue's seam, and on 2026-11-15 ruled the resource and the ceiling's figure; the Factory Builder ruled where the parser lives and who counts the day's spend. It adds a reader to the review's Run, a second transport, a key, and the `annotation` surface to decision 0145's row "An adopted Finding's surface". It adds a second file to decision 0044's one transport. It changes nothing in what the Kernel decides, in decision 0092, or in the Engine. It is built in parts, and a row of the Boundaries is added with the check that holds it.

## Why

The second-family read was the one check on a change that neither the builder's family wrote nor the builder's family judged, and it was the only one the Factory did not hold: it ran when a session chose, on what a worktree held, and its result was a line of prose in the change's own description. Moving the reader inside the Run makes it a read of the admitted commit that the author cannot skip, select from or summarise. Keeping it advisory keeps a reader whose majors are fairly major 11 times in 27 away from every decision. `pr-review` already reads the head with the read tools alone (decision 0138, as 0147 holds it), so the step that makes a reader's finding trustworthy, checking it against the code, needs no new tool and no new Role.

What is owed: what Azure keeps of a response and for how long, before the first private Consumer is served; the trials that comment proposes, an answer's states, its refusals, its time, a check run's annotations, a hostile diff and Kimi's answer, none of which has run; Kimi's price, which the desk assumed; and the replay.
