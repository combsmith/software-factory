# 0242. Implementing rounds have a limit of their own, and the first opening push lands on the default branch's head its admission carries

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's rulings of 2026-11-20, from the Solution Architect's, on "An implementing Run's attempt bound, and the base commit the first opening push is held to" (issue 1038), the follow-on to issue 952: the two points that decision 0239, "Push is granted only where no secret-bearing job runs what a patch may change, no Run holds a tool that runs code, and a later opening Run opens from the branch", left to a record of their own. It gives decision 0219's row "One round, one Run" the bound for `implement` it raised, reads decision 0217's Hold of the implementing rounds at `max_attempts` as a Hold at that bound, and says for decision 0188's list and decision 0238's opening where the first opening push is read and where it lands, on decision 0226's `base_head`, the default branch's head the opening admission carries. The rest of 0217, 0219, 0226, 0238 and 0239 stands.

## Why

Decision 0239 left two points of the reader audit of 0219 unsettled. The implementing rounds were bounded by `max_attempts`, which 0219 said would be raised for `implement` alone, though it is one number for every Intent's attempts; a key of their own lets the rounds be raised without raising every other loop, and a check keeps that key under the Run ceiling. And the first opening push had no base commit for 0188's list or for the patch to land on; the default branch's head, carried in the Run's admission, is that base, held to the default branch's history at the push, with both commits' lists read so that neither a protected path added since nor one taken off since lets a patch through.
