# 0083. A Lens judges, and the queue App resolves its thread as the Kernel's act on its accepted ruling

Status: accepted. The Maintainer's decision of 2026-10-29, as the Factory Builder gave it for "A Lens's thread is resolved by a Kernel act the queue App carries out, on that Lens's accepted ruling, never by the Lens's own App" (issue 389), with the Builder's rulings on its five choices. It changes the closing rule of decision 0007's row "A person's thread resolved by anyone but that person stays open", the resolver and reconciliation rows of decision 0067, the join of decision 0069 and the queue's needs of decision 0081; it narrows decision 0005, since no Role's App resolves a thread; and it notes decision 0075's "A problem that still stands".

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Who judges | The Lens, as today: the owner of a Finding's category decides it `accepted` or `rejected` in its Result (design section 3.8), and `apply` records each decision it takes in the Run's `applied/1`, as its `rulings`: the Finding, the thread it is on, and the result | A Disposition was read only from the thread's state; resolving the thread was the only record of an acceptance |
| Who resolves | No Role. `resolve_thread` leaves `effect/1`, `apply`'s writes and the pr-review App's grant: a Result that asks for it is no Result. Resolving a Lens's thread is the Kernel's act, `resolve_thread`, which the queue App, `combsmith-sf-queue`, carries out | Resolving another's thread takes `contents: write` (trials/P-5.json, decision 0082), which no reviewer's App holds and the queue App does; and no Role closes a Finding by resolving it (L-17) |
| When a Lens's thread is closed | Resolved, and resolved by a person with write access, or by the queue App with that Lens's latest ruling on it `accepted`. The Lens's own App, the author's and any other App close nothing; a ruling of none or `rejected` leaves the queue App's resolution open. A person's thread is closed only by that person, as before | The Lens judges and the Kernel acts: a resolution means the judgement only when the act made it |
| The ruling, held to its Lens | `adapters/applied.py` joins a Lens's thread's `ruling` from that Lens's own records, as its category is joined: the result of its latest Run that ruled on the thread, by the Run's start. None where it ruled on nothing, ruled twice in that Run, a start cannot be read, or the Lens does not own the thread's category. A record not read whole leaves the threads not whole, and the Kernel defers | A ruling the port could not hold to its owner is no ruling |
| A ruling the Kernel cannot read | A thread with no `ruling`, or one that is neither `accepted`, `rejected` nor null, is unreadable, and nothing converges on it | It decides whether a resolution closes the thread (I-13) |
| Who the queue App is | The account `controller/1` holds as `accounts.queue`, by id; `adapters/timeline.Accounts` types it `queue`, a kind of its own. Null until the App is registered ("A narrow queue App, combsmith-sf-queue, enqueues and dequeues with contents and pull-request write alone (P-2)", issue 381), and while it is, no account is typed `queue` and no resolution closes a Lens's thread but a person's | A kind is told by the configuration and the account, never a name (L-57) |
| The act | The Kernel decides `resolve_thread` (reason `resolve_due`, State adjudicating) for every Lens thread GitHub shows open whose ruling is `accepted`, the threads its evidence. It comes before the author is sent back and before any gate holds. The brakes hold it as any act: a Pause stops it and the act ceiling holds it. The Controller records it `not_built` until the queue App's step exists (decision 0051) | The Lens judged it; only the act closes it |
| Counting it | A landed resolution is counted once the step that makes it exists (issue 381): its job is a dispatched run, as the approving job is, read whole from the run records at every call. Until then none lands, so none goes uncounted | GitHub gives no time for a resolution, and it is no timeline event; the pass reads its own writes only for its window, and its records for the last two passes |
| The queue | A queued item with a gating thread open, new or still standing, accepted or not, leaves the queue first: the queue App's dequeue, before anything else. A queued item reads past `resolve_due`, so one whose accepted threads gate nothing stays | The queue checks that threads are resolved only as an item enters it: it merged canary PR 70 at 02:34:42Z with a thread opened after (window 3). The ruleset is never relied on for it |
| Reconciliation | A resolution is the queue App's, matched to a `resolve_thread` write of the pass by the thread's id. Any Role's resolution alarms, whatever it recorded. Until the queue App's account is set it is no identity, and leaves nothing short | Every write a Factory identity makes is matched to a recorded Effect (decision 0061) |
| The needs | `effect:resolve_thread` waits on the queue App, as `effect:enqueue_merge` does; the pr-review App no longer holds it | A need belongs to one App (`tools/check_apps.py`) |
| A security Finding | `apply` refuses an accepted decision on a security thread, `security_proposal_unread`, as it refused to resolve one: the author's proposal, which alone may make it acceptable, is in a Run `apply` cannot read yet | A security Finding is never accepted as declined or deferred (decision 0007) |
| A problem that still stands | There is no act that marks a thread unresolved. A Lens that finds a resolved problem still stands raises it again, on a new thread of its own (decision 0075) | Its fresh record holds what it raised |
| The Lifecycle | A Lens's own App resolving its thread no longer moves an item to converged, so `addressing`, `adjudicating` and `held` to `converged` by a Lens are gone, and so are the four Holds only that move made. The act ceiling on an accepted ruling (`addressing` to `held` by the Factory) and the Run ceiling after a Lens's App resolves an accepted thread out of turn (`adjudicating` to `held` by a Lens) are declared | Each transition the Kernel makes is declared, and none it no longer makes (I-12) |
| The fake | The simulator's owner rules in its record and resolves nothing; the queue App resolves a Lens's thread on the Kernel's act (`an_app_resolves_any_thread`). `an_app_resolves_a_lens_thread`, which P-5 contradicted, is gone, since the fake no longer applies it | A rule is grounded only where the fake does what the fixture shows (L-51) |
| Trials owed | P-5 again, with the queue App, typing its `resolvedBy`; whether the queue refuses or ejects a pull request with a thread not resolved; and the branch policy of the queue's environment. The trials lane runs them | What GitHub does with the queue App is read, never assumed |

## Why

A Lens's own App resolved its threads, and the gate read that as an accepted Disposition. P-5 showed the reviewer's App cannot resolve a thread another opened, and P-25 that a reviewer approves once its thread is resolved: who resolves decides what a resolution means. The Lens keeps the judgement, recorded where the port can hold it to the Lens, and the Kernel resolves the thread as its own act, through the one App that may.
