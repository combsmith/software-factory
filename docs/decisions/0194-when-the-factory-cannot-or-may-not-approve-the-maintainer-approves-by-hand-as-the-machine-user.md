# 0194. When the Factory cannot or may not approve, the Maintainer approves by hand as the machine user

Status: accepted. Working ruling 5 on "The ruleset switch: main requires the machine user's approval of the most recent commit, and slice 1's event is recorded" (issue 198), and the Maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-11, as the Factory Builder relayed it, that a change to a model Lens's input goes through by his hand: he approves its head as the machine user in a browser, and it is enqueued through his login. It says what 0008's "The Maintainer's own approval never depends on the Factory running" and 0052's "the Maintainer's merge by hand outside the Factory" mean while he authors every pull request, and carries out 0191's row "What is not shown yet" as far as a record can.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| The Maintainer's own approval | While he authors every pull request, it is his approval by hand as the machine user, in a browser signed in to that account; his login then enqueues the pull request, and `verify` on the merge group is still required. Nobody is given a bypass | GitHub counts no approval from an author (0191), so his own login cannot give it, and a bypass is what `examples/invalid/rulesets.main-with-a-bypass.json` refuses. A hand approval needs no Release, no Run and no stored token, so it does not depend on the Factory running |
| A Release that cannot approve | First the Pause, which holds the Factory still. Then a rollback of `next`, if an earlier Release served: the Pause is lifted, and that Release approves the fix. The hand approval is only for when no Release can serve; then the Pause stays until a Release cut from the fix is on `next`, and that cut is the Maintainer's own dispatch, made under the Pause | A rollback leaves the Factory doing the approving, and recording it as its own Effect. A hand approval is one the Factory did not record, so it comes last. Lifting the Pause before the new Release is on `next` would let the broken one serve again |
| A change the Factory may not approve | A change to a model Lens's input merges on the same hand approval, with no rollback, kept small and alone in its pull request. The Kernel still counts no person's approval of it and never converges it, and its Decision on it in the merge queue is to take it out. Nothing carries that Decision out while `config/controller.json` names no queue account, so no Pause is needed until one is named; from then the hand merge is made under a Pause, which alone stops a dequeue | 0052 gives his own change to those paths two ways, a colleague's approval or his merge by hand outside the Factory, and this is the second. The dequeue is 0052's too, held by `tests/test_decide.py::test_a_model_review_alone_never_suffices`; a merge that raced it would be no rule |
| What follows a hand approval | The Controller's reconciliation reads it as a write by a Factory identity with no recorded Effect and raises the alert `write_unmatched`, naming the review; its merge may read `merged_by_override`. The Maintainer matches the alert's entry to the approval he gave. The alert stays as it is | 0061: every approval by the machine user with no Effect alarms, his own in the browser included, by `tests/test_reconcile.py::test_every_machine_user_approval_with_no_record_alarms_the_maintainers_own_included`. That match is the one thing that tells his own act from another's use of the login |
| What a session does | It stops before the approval and hands him the pull request, its head, and the reads to make before and after. It approves nothing, signs in to no account and sets no variable. The skill `.claude/skills/cut-a-release/SKILL.md` says so, word for word as its test names it, and gives nothing to run | The machine user's login is his alone, and a Pause is a person's control |
| What is shown elsewhere | That a fix does merge this way with the Factory paused is shown once, on a pull request, and what was read of it is kept on the slice's epic, not in this repo | A showing is an event, not a rule, and no check here reads GitHub |

## Why

From the switch, `main` merges nothing without a code owner's approval of the last push (0191), and the approval that counts for the Maintainer's own pull requests is the machine user's. If the only way to give it were the Factory's approving act, a broken Release could not be mended through `main`, which is what 0008 forbids, and a change to what a model Lens is given could not merge at all, since the Factory is never to approve one (0052). One act answers both: his own hand, in the machine user's name. It is kept for last when a Release is merely broken, because a rollback keeps the Factory's record whole and a hand approval is one more alert to match.
