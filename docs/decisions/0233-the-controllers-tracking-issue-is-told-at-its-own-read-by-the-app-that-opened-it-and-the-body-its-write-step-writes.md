# 0233. The Controller's tracking issue is told at its own read, by the App that opened it and the body its write step writes

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's ruling of 2026-11-19 on "The tracking issue's number stays known after its write leaves the records' window" (issue 931), option (A) narrowed. For the issue's read alone, it changes the Factory Builder's ruling of 2026-11-18 that the tracking issue is left out "by the number the passes recorded writing it under, never by its author", on "The issues port drops a comment by a Role App held only under `roles`, and a queue's or Lens's name declared as an App's refuses that App's real comment" (issue 901), which decision 0218's row on the tracking issue holds. The listing still leaves it out by that number, and 0218 stands.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| What is told | An issue is the Controller's tracking issue when two things hold at the issues port's first REST read of it: the Controller's App opened it, by the configured account's id and type, and its body is one the write step writes, by the write step's own test, `causes_of` in `adapters/effects.py`. The issues port refuses it there, `Tracking`, and sends nothing more for it | The number the passes record is known only while the write is inside the records' window, and is never recorded for an opening that went unanswered or was answered unmatched, nor for two open tracking issues. The issue itself says who opened it and what its body is, at every pass, whatever the records hold |
| Not by its author alone | An issue the Controller's App opened with any other body is a Work item, and is served. So is one whose body anyone edited | Later Roles open follow-on issues the Factory must serve, and the write step itself treats only a body it wrote as its tracking issue |
| What the pass does with it | It is neither decided nor left out: it was never a Work item, as one the listing leaves out by its number is not. The served issues' writes are read for reconciliation by "The Controller's writes on issues are reconciled before issues are served" (issue 837, decision 0230), which hands that read the issues decided or left out, so never one told here: its writes are not read | The Factory never triages its own alert (the Factory Builder's ruling of 2026-11-18) |
| What cannot be read | An issue whose opener cannot be read is refused, and is left out as unread, never served. A tracking issue whose first read fails is left out as unread too | An unreadable fact yields a deferral, never an action |
| No App named | With no Controller App named in the Controller's configuration, no issue is the tracking issue. So one opened while the App was named, or under another account, is served once no record in the window numbers it, if its App is later unnamed or renumbered: accepted, since only a change to the configuration does it | The write step writes no tracking issue while the App is unnamed (`tracking` in `adapters/effects.py`) |
| What stays | The listing leaves out every number the passes recorded (decision 0218's row), and while the passes' records are not read whole no issue is read. Each is kept as it is | Neither costs a read, and the second holds while the records are short, whichever way the tracking issue is told |
| The residual | A served issue's writes are read whether its facts were read or not (decision 0230). So in a pass where the tracking issue's own read fails and no record numbers it, it is left out as unread, its writes are read beside the served issues', and the Controller's assignment there can be `write_unmatched` for that pass. So too in a pass whose records were not read whole, where every issue listed is left out as unread (decision 0218's row) | Loud, never silent, and only while its read fails or the records are short, when reconciliation is short too |

## Why

"The tracking issue's number stays known after its write leaves the records' window" (issue 931) asks that the tracking issue stay apart for as long as it is open, derived at each pass and never stored apart. The pass records keep its number for the window alone, and keep none at all for the openings that went unanswered or were answered unmatched. The issue's own read is already made for every issue listed, and REST's `user` there is the account that opened it, so telling the tracking issue there costs no request and covers every case the recorded number leaves open. Its body, written by the write step and held line for line by the write step's test, keeps the App's other issues served.
