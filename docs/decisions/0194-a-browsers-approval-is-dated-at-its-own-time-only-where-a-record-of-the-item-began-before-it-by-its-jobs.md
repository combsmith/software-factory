# 0194. A browser's approval is dated at its own time only where a record of the item began before it, by its jobs

Status: accepted. The Factory Builder's ruling of 2026-11-11 on issue 724, "A record's start is read from its job's started_at, trusted only once the job is in progress or ran (P-64)". It amends three rows of `docs/decisions/0168-the-simulators-world-dates-an-approval-by-the-acts-run-and-the-kernels-anchor-takes-the-records-always.md`: "A browser's approval", "One nothing anchors" and "The residuals", whose second residual it closes. The rest of 0168 stands. It reads the field `docs/decisions/0193-when-a-run-began-is-read-from-its-jobs-trusted-only-once-a-job-is-in-progress-or-ran-and-kept-by-the-pass-records.md` gave the run record.

## Decision

| What | Decided | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A browser's approval | A machine user's approval with no act's Run on its content made at or before it is measured from its own time only where a record of this item began at or before it: a record whose `began_at` is a time no later than the approval and no earlier than when the record was made | 0168 took a record's `started_at` for when it began, and that is when its run was made (`trials/P-44.json`). A Run made while the approving act ran waits in the item's lane and begins after the act ends, so by its jobs' start it is later than the approval the act made (`trials/P-64.json`) |
| A record that says no beginning | One with no `began_at`, with null, or with one that is no time does not reach back, and never raises. With no other record as old as the approval, any edit holds the item until a person approves after it, as 0168 rules for one nothing anchors | It has not begun, never began, or its jobs were not read. None shows that a record older than the act's Run is still kept. Nor does one said to have begun before it was made, its dispatch or its start: no run begins before it is made, and a re-run keeps its dispatch, so its first attempt's beginning stands |
| The act's Run | Unchanged: dated by when it was made, its dispatch or its start. A record whose start cannot be read still holds any edit | The act reads the description after its Run is made, so when it was made is the early side |
| The residual it closes | 0168's second: a Run made while the act ran, once the act's Run is past retention and it is not, but for an approving Run on the content, named below. Such a Run began after the act's job ended, which is after its approval | In P-64 the job that waited began a second after the first ended; in `trials/P-55.json`, 4 seconds |
| What remains | An act's Run a person deletes by hand while an older record stays, as 0168 names. An approving Run on the content made while the act ran, a second dispatch or a re-run in the act's own seconds, is still taken for the act by when it was made, whenever it began: the pass makes no such Run, and a re-run's jobs say when its last attempt began, so its beginning cannot rule it out. A Run that began in the approval's own second reaches back, as 0168 rules for a second. A job outside its item's lane, as 0193 names. And a list read with no job asked gives no beginning, so every browser's approval with an edit holds there | One second cannot be ordered. The last fails closed |
| The simulator | The world's records say when they began: a Run is made when it is admitted and begins when its lane starts it, and one cancelled while it waited never began. The issue's world says nothing of a beginning, as `kernel/issue.py` reads none. `read_at` takes a run as old as the approval by when it began. The fake's rule `a_run_begins_with_its_job` is grounded on P-64 | The world's reading of an edit must be the Kernel's from the same records. `sim/world.py` and `tests/test_simulator.py` are protected: pre-approved by the Maintainer 2026-11-08, ruled by the Factory Builder 2026-11-11 |
| What it reads | A run record's `began_at`. `facts/1` gains no field, so the held-out cases are unchanged | The record already carries it |

## Why

Decision 0168 left one way for an edit to pass unseen that no person's act causes: the retention's edge falling in the act's own seconds, with a Run made in those seconds still kept. That Run's record said it started before the approval, because a record's start is its creation. P-64 showed what says when it began, and decision 0193 reads it.
